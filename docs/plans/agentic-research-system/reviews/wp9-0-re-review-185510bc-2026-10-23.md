## 4. The two points the author asked about

### 4.1 Is the reference tool within P-065's "Docs and data files only"? (new finding N-1, Major, owner decision)

**Read literally, no.** P-065's Boundary line reads "Docs and data files only", and its **Not authorized** list
includes "any runtime code". `tools/w9_segmentation.py` is not runtime code: it lives outside `research_system/`,
opens no store, writes nothing, and its tests are synthetic. But it is code, not a document or data file. It is also,
in substance, a first implementation of the contract that 06v assigns to WP9.3 ("WP9.3 implements it").

**Why it should probably stay.** My first review's M-8 asked for "a minimal reference script and its vectors
committed with the contract". D-7 needs a reader for the L-0b export before WP9.3 exists. The tool's vectors are also
the only mechanical evidence that the contract text is unambiguous, which is the contract's whole purpose.

**Required.** This is Stephen's decision, and the reconciliation correctly leaves it to him (§3.1). His acceptance of
revision 2 should name the two files exactly: `tools/w9_segmentation.py` and `tests/tools/test_w9_segmentation.py`.
It should rule either:
- (a) that they are within WP9.0, as the contract's reference vectors; or
- (b) that they move to WP9.3.

Recommendation: (a), recorded as an explicit, named extension of P-065's boundary to those two non-runtime files. A
reading by implication is not enough. Under (b), D-7's L-0b export has no reader until WP9.3.

### N-2 — Minor: frontmatter line 1, where the tool and a vector contradict the contract

**Evidence.**
- Contract §3, the `log_frontmatter` row: "Line 1, when its match text is exactly `---`".
- `tools/w9_segmentation.py` `segment_frontmatter` tests `match_text(lines[0]).rstrip(b" \t") != b"---"`, which trims
  trailing spaces and tabs.
- The vector `(b"---  \r\na: b\r\n---\r\n", [("item", 1, 3, …)])` pins the trimmed behaviour.

Under the contract, the trimmed case is a blocker. Since "the contract governs" (§7), the tool and the vector are wrong
as the text stands.

**Required change.** Amend the contract row to "Line 1, when its match text, trimmed of trailing spaces and tabs, is
exactly `---`". That matches the closing delimiter and §2.5's rule-line definition, and none of the 85 stage logs
changes. The alternative is to fix the tool and the vector.

### N-3 — Minor: §7 says the tool "implements every rule above", but two contract rules are not in it

**Evidence.**
- **Suppressed anchors.** Contract §3 says a suppressed anchor "opens no item, and the inventory records it".
  `segment_heading_sections` drops declared lines silently and emits no row for them.
- **Covered region.** Contract §3 declares the covered region as "a line range plus the SHA-256 of each anchor line's
  bytes", or "from the first anchor after a named structural line, given by line number and hash". The tool takes a
  bare `--region START:END` and verifies no anchor-line hash. It does not implement the second form.

**Required change.** Either implement both, which is small, or narrow §7 to say that region declarations and the
recording of suppressed anchors are inventory-level checks that the tool does not perform.

### N-4 — Minor: §6.3 gives the wrong reason for carrying live-page offsets

**Evidence.**
- §6.3 says entries carry live-page offsets "so the vault detector … can locate each entry in the live page".
- New entries go at the top of the page. Between the census (`de44a67b`, 700,632 bytes, title at L4436) and this
  re-review's read about an hour later (703,531 bytes), one entry was added and the title moved to L4476. Every legacy
  entry's offset moved with it.

**Required change.** State that the detector locates entries by identity (anchor text), and that offsets reproduce
the export's own read only.

### N-5 — Minor: the L-0b copy must be retained, and the recheck's meaning stated

**Evidence.**
- §6.1 reads the export "from bytes copied once from the live page", and §6.2 has WP9.3 "re-hash the same bytes".
  Neither says where the copy is kept, or that its hash is Git-anchored with the export (W9 §7.3 item 2 anchors only
  the export).
- The recheck can prove that the segmentation reproduces on that copy. It cannot retroactively turn a path-string read
  into a handle-bound one.

**Required change.**
- Retain the copy, or at least its SHA-256 and size, in the same commit as the export.
- State that WP9.3's recheck verifies reproducibility of the segmentation, not the identity of the original read. That
  limit stays recorded, as in D8c §7.

### N-6 — Minor: the export should carry the non-item rows too

**Evidence.**
- §6 makes the export carry "every entry". The tool emits structural and blocker rows with their own
  `raw_row_sha256`; on today's page, that is the title block.
- Bytes in a structural block of the legacy region belong to no entry. An edit to them would not change any entry
  hash, so W9-T19 would miss it.

**Required change.** The export carries every row the tool emits for the legacy region (items, structural blocks and
blockers), each with its hash and offsets. The vault detector compares all of them.
