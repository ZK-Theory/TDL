## Repository Workflow

Long-form workflow guidance and research conventions are kept outside the root policy file.
Use these instructions when working on paper drafts, vault integration, or research process rules.

### Key guidance

- Keep paper drafts versioned in `papers/PXX/drafts/`.
- Do not overwrite existing draft versions.
- Update the relevant `papers/PXX/_project.md` open items when work is completed.
- Save quantitative outputs to `results/` and follow the repository's results logging conventions.

- `.claude/instructions/research-context.instructions.md` for new script headers and research metadata.
