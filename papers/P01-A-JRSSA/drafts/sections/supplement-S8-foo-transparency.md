# §S8 Family-of-Origin Transparency for Conditional Escape Models

The attempted cross-classified family-of-origin GLMM is not used as a headline model. The fitted-stage reconstruction for the disadvantaged-starter model contains 6,995 observations, of which 6,284 are FOO singletons and only 711 are members of multi-member FOO clusters. Those 711 respondents are distributed over 342 multi-member clusters. Earlier counts of 7,098 total observations, 6,363 singletons, 735 multi-members, and 353 multi-member clusters referred to a pre-complete-case eligibility stage and should not be reported as fitted-sample counts.

The singleton decomposition clarifies why the cross-classified variance component is weakly identified. Among the 6,284 fitted-stage singletons, 4,350 are true singletons and 1,934 are filtered singletons whose siblings exist in the underlying FOO data but do not remain in the fitted disadvantaged-starter frame. The primary filter behind the latter group is failing the first-window R2/R6 starter restriction (1,932 cases), with only two cases primarily removed by zero IPW eligibility. This means the fitted model frame contains very little within-FOO replication for estimating a family-origin random intercept.

The within-family evidence among the retained multi-member subset is also limited. The corrected all-pairs sibling concordance analysis uses 396 pairs across the 342 multi-member clusters. Its 2 x 2 table is (2, 14, 25, 355), Cohen's kappa is 0.0445 with bootstrap CI [-0.0552, 0.1952], McNemar's asymptotic p-value is 0.1093, and the odds ratio is 2.0286 with bootstrap CI [0.6390, 8.2653]. These numbers are consistent with a small and uncertain concordance signal, not with a stable variance-component basis for a cross-classified headline.

The corrected power simulation reinforces the same conclusion. At ICC = 0, the nominal 5% test rejected in 951 of 1,000 simulations (type-I rate = 0.951), so the engine is not calibrated. The reported `minimum_detectable_icc` is therefore "engine not calibrated". The multi-member-only sigma_foo estimate (6.2319; bootstrap CI [1.4886, 7.8032]) is treated as a separation/non-estimability artifact, not as evidence of a large substantive FOO variance component.

A full-sample sensitivity analysis, outside the R2/R6 fitted-starter frame, estimates sigma_foo = 0.1702 in 27,252 observations across 18,524 FOO clusters, with a 95% cluster-bootstrap CI [0.0001, 36.9459]. The interval is technically strictly above zero, but it is far too wide to sustain a strong variance-component claim. It is best reported as a disclosure that family-origin variance is detectable only in a fragile, specification-sensitive sense, while the main-text §4.5 family-of-origin contribution comes from the local trajectory-geometry analysis and its non-topology-specific comparator checks.
